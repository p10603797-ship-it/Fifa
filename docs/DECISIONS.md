# Decisions

Decisions taken where the brief is silent or the environment forces a choice. Newest last.

| Id | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D-001 | TypeScript 5.9.3 (not 6.x/7.x) | typescript-eslint 8.71 supports < 6.1; 7.x is the native compiler without the JS API that tooling expects. |
| D-002 | Vite 8.3.4, Vitest 5.0.4, Playwright 1.56.1, Three 0.186.1, PeerJS 1.5.5, vite-plugin-singlefile 2.3.3; fonts from @fontsource 5.3.0 (OFL) | Pinned current stable versions; Playwright 1.56.1 matches the container's preinstalled Chromium build 1194. |
| D-003 | Only Chromium exists in the build container (no WebKit/Firefox binaries, and installing browsers is not allowed). Playwright WebKit project auto-enables when a WebKit build is present. Determinism is verified in Node and Chromium here; cross-engine safety comes from the deterministic math module (no transcendental Math calls in sim/ai). | Environment limit. Re-scoped in TEST_REPORT; the device checklist covers Safari. |
| D-004 | InputFrame buttons are positional-semantic (FC Classic positions: PASS=Cross/A, SHOOT=Circle/B, LOB=Square/X, THROUGH=Triangle/Y, MOD_L=L1, MOD_R=R1, SHIELD=L2, SPRINT=R2) with context aliases (TACKLE, SLIDE, SWITCH, ...). The sim resolves meaning from context. | Keeps one input format, lets holds/combos/possession swaps resolve deterministically in the sim, and touch maps onto the same bits. |
| D-005 | Agents share one working tree with exclusive file ownership; the lead commits between batches and runs the verify gate. No per-agent git worktrees. | 4-CPU container; later agents build on real implementations instead of stubs; exclusive ownership makes merges trivial anyway. |
| D-006 | SIM-15 split into SIM-15 (action input resolution) and SIM-16 (player switching). Roster total 201. | Both are large; switching is a self-contained module. |
| D-007 | 22 fixed on-pitch slots (team 0: 0-10, team 1: 11-21). A substitution swaps the roster index and attributes into the slot; a sent-off player's slot becomes inactive. | Fixed-size typed state, simple invariants. |
| D-008 | Restarts on the main camera (throw-ins, free kicks more than 35 m from the attacked goal) are walked: from the award the taker runs to his spot and illegal players walk out; the set-up becomes ready once everyone is in place (at the earliest restartSetupTicks); stragglers are placed setupStragglerMaxTicks later, or up to setupExtendMaxTicks while the taker is still running in. Corners, goal kicks, penalties and free kicks within 35 m keep the instant placement at restartSetupTicks, hidden by the set-piece camera cut; kick-offs are placed at phase entry during the cut-scene. (Revised by QF-10; originally every restart placed players instantly.) | Guarantees legal restarts and no "blocking forever" glitches without visible teleports. |
| D-009 | Saves are always made by the keeper AI, also on human sides; humans control rush-out, pick-up and distribution. | Matches console behaviour and the brief's GKP split. |
| D-010 | The lead wrote the toolchain (FND-01) and contracts + sim/ai skeleton (FND-02) directly. | They are the frozen foundation every other assignment depends on. |
| D-011 | Pitch-space input: stick right = +x, stick down = +z for the broadcast camera; other cameras rotate via CameraProjector.stickRotation(). | Predictable control under a fixed side camera; network frames are camera-independent. |
| D-012 | POSSESSION events only on a change of team (a loose ball is judged against the last touch). | Completed passes must not look like turnovers to stats, crowd and HUD. |
| D-013 | The match clock does not run before a kick-off is taken; it runs in play and other restarts (added time from restart phases). PRE_MATCH may go straight to IN_PLAY (practice free play, tests). | Real-football feel for 2-10 minute halves; deterministic e2e entry. |
| D-014 | Camera FOV stays 22 deg: at the iPad aspect a player at the centre spot is 5.7% of screen height with 43.6 m visible (the 6% / 45 m targets pull against each other). | FND-05 measurement; GFX-14 may retune. |
| D-015 | E2E runs at deviceScaleFactor 1 by default (PW_HIDPI=1 adds DPR 2) with per-agent build dirs and ports; vitest maxWorkers 2. | Software WebGL in the 4-CPU container; many agents share it. |
| D-016 | Possession band is judged per match: mean share of the side with more of the ball <= 60%. | FND-06 reading of "40-60% for each side". |
| D-017 | From the second resume onward, remaining Wave 1 assignments are merged into one agent per related group (e.g. GFX-09..12 animation, AUD-01..05 audio) and the per-assignment independent check pass is dropped; verification moves to Wave 2 integration and a lean QA wave. Assignment ids, briefs and ownership are unchanged. | The account's weekly usage limit was reached twice; merged agents load shared context once. |
| D-018 | Touch PASS swipe left inside the crossing zone sends two LOB taps (resolved as GROUND_CROSS); outside the zone it stays MOD_L+MOD_R+LOB (driven lob). R1+PASS inside the zone is a low cross. | Resolves the §9 vs §10 conflict raised by SIM-15 without device-specific logic in the sim. |
| D-019 | A disconnected controller's side can be handed to its CPU brain with Command.HAND_TO_CPU (cmdArg = human) carried in that human's frame; handled in sim/control/handoff.ts before the frame is resolved. SessionControl.setHumanDevice(human, devices or 'cpu') rebinds devices locally; it returns false online. | Keeps the hand-off deterministic and in replays; PAD-03 reconnect card needs it. |
| D-020 | Waves 3 and 4 are merged per area: eight QA+fix agents (QF-1..QF-8) each hunt defects in their charter and fix them in files they own, keeping the QA-xx/FIX-xx ids; performance (QF-7) runs last and alone. | Usage limits (D-017); a finder that also fixes avoids a second context load per defect. Ownership stays exclusive per area. |
| D-021 | MatchSetup.hooks (allowPause, onPauseChange, onQuit) lets a mode enforce its own pause rules and hear about quits; the session consults them. Online 1v1 (NET-06) uses them for 3 pauses each / 30 s cap / only the pauser resumes, and Finish against CPU uses Command.HAND_TO_CPU for the absent human (D-019). | Keeps the app session mode-agnostic and local play free of network code (§11). |
| D-022 | The difficulty ladder and club balance targets are kept as written: "beats" means wins (a draw is not a win), measured bot-v-bot at the default 4-minute halves. Levels and ratings must create real differences in play to reach them, never physics or stat boosts. | The QF-2 measurement showed the targets are not met because levels barely change results; changing the reading would hide that. |
