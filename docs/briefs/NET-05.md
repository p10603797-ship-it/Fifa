Transport is PeerJS (peerjs 1.5.5) over WebRTC data channels with the free public PeerJS broker and default ICE servers, settings in one config object; no keys; only a nickname and inputs are sent; no chat, no accounts. Deterministic lockstep with input delay (3 ticks initially, adaptive 2-8), inputs on an unordered channel with the last 10 frames repeated in every packet, lobby/pause/resync on a reliable channel, late input holds the sim with a small waiting indicator and never guesses, desync guard: state hash every 30 ticks, host sends a snapshot on mismatch, guest hard-syncs, both log seed/tick/inputs. Tests must not touch the public network: inject a fake transport (in-memory pair with configurable latency/jitter/loss from NET-08).
Implement: every 30 ticks each peer sends (tick, hashState) on the reliable channel; compare when both are known; on mismatch: host sends a full snapshot
(serializeState at a tick both have reached, chunked if large, compressed with CompressionStream where available), guest hard-syncs (deserialize, replace
state, align tick and input queues), both log seed/tick/inputs/diff (diffState if the guest can compute it) into diagnostics; after resync verify the next
hash. Expose stats (desyncs, last resync tick).
Tests: injected divergence (flip a field on the guest) is detected within 30 ticks and repaired; snapshot round trip; no false positives over a long run.
