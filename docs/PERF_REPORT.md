## Final benchmark (SGN-04)

Measured 2026-10-23 on a fresh production build of the final tree: `npx vite build --outDir dist-e2e/sgn-04
--emptyOutDir && node tools/postbuild.mjs dist-e2e/sgn-04` (what `npm run build` does, in a build folder of its own;
postbuild PASS). Chromium 141 headless at the iPad viewport 1180 x 820, CDP CPU throttling where stated.

**This container is not an iPad.** It has 4 shared Xeon cores at 2.8 GHz (load average 2-3.8 from other agents during
these runs) and no GPU: WebGL is SwiftShader, a software rasteriser that draws a frame in 100-500 ms. Frame rates
with real drawing say nothing about an A16 GPU, so the frame-time runs stub the WebGL draw calls (`--nodraw`: three.js
still does all its JavaScript, state and upload work and counts the draw calls) and measure the main thread. CDP
throttling slows the whole main thread, garbage collection included, and is not exactly linear: sim code run
back to back is 6-8x slower at "4x" than at 1x here. V8 is not JavaScriptCore either: V8 boxes doubles in its lower
tiers, and that boxing is where most bytes per tick come from (below); JavaScriptCore NaN-boxes doubles. The fps and
frame-time targets, and whether the sim budget holds on the iPad, are decided only by the on-device benchmark
(`?bench=1`, DEVICE_CHECKLIST step 2). WebKit and Firefox are not installed here: NOT VERIFIABLE HERE.

### Open items for the lead (SGN-04)

1. The proxy gate fails on sim time at 4x: `tools/bench.ts` exits 1 (average 2.97 ms, p99 11.9 ms), and it still fails
   with drawing stubbed (3.2-3.6 ms, p99 14.6-17.1 ms). No simple defect causes it: the sim's back-to-back cost is
   0.22-0.32 ms per tick at 1x. The choice is to accept the device benchmark as the judge (DoD 6 stays open until the
   iPad result comes back) or to cut the paced per-tick cost by about a third (the AI's evaluation volume, and the V8
   boxing that triggers minor GCs inside ticks).
2. Benchmark seed: change `BENCH_DEFAULTS.seed` in src/platform/benchHook.ts from `0xb3ac4` to `11`, so the measured
   window holds a corner, a goal and its replay (BRIEF section 13, and what DEVICE_CHECKLIST step 2 already says).
   Then make `tests/perf/sgn-04-bench-coverage.ts` a test (`coverage(BENCH_DEFAULTS.seed).ok`): any sim change
   reshapes the match, and the test catches a seed that no longer covers the cases.
3. `npx tsx tools/size-check.ts` exits 1: the src/contracts group is 110.5 KB against its 80 KB early-warning budget
   (TUN-2 moved every gameplay constant into src/contracts/tuning.ts). The 4 MB total is at 20 %; raise that group
   budget in tools/size-check.ts (for example to 160 KB).
4. `textureMemory()` (src/render/index.ts) does not count a shadow map's depth texture (4 MB at Quality). The budget
   is unaffected (14 MB of 64).

### 1. Allocation per tick (V8)

Method: `tests/perf/tun-7s-sim-alloc-probe.ts` in a child process with the QF-7R exact flags (`--single-threaded`,
